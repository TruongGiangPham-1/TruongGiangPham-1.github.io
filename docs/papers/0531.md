## Aside note
"MAs in optimization are really weird since they don't tend to improve asymptotics, just typcial behaviour:
For instance, consider the Frank-Wolfe methods for constrained convex optimization.
In constrained optimization there are two behaviours:
First you move through the space towards the boundary (if the solution is not at the boundary, you effectively have an unconstraind problem).
Second, once you are at the boundary, you have to navigate towards the optimum.

the major difference between momentum and non-momentum in Frank-Wolfe is just how quickly you can find the boundary.
This is really important in practice, but doesn't actually change your asymptotics since those are dominated by the barrier-following.
"
